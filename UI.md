Design a modern, elegant, and highly professional church website UI that feels calm, trustworthy, and spiritually uplifting without looking outdated or overly decorative.

The visual style must be minimal, refined, and beautiful with strong hierarchy, generous spacing, and excellent typography. Use a dark theme with subtle contrast, soft lighting, and a restrained color palette (charcoal, soft gray, and a dignified accent like muted blue or gold).

The layout should feel advanced, clean, and premium — similar to high-end tech or design agency websites — while still warm and welcoming for visitors of all ages.

Prioritize clarity, readability, and intuitive navigation. The design must communicate credibility, peace, and reverence. Avoid clutter, bright colors, or dated church-style visuals.

Include sections for: Home, About the Church, Sermons, Events, Ministries, Give/Donate, and Contact.

Use large hero imagery with soft gradients, elegant serif/sans-serif font pairing, smooth spacing, modern cards, subtle shadows, and gentle animations.

The result should feel beautiful, advanced, and professional — something that reflects excellence, faith, and trust at first glance.
